01 • THE TERRIBLE IDEA

We Had Exactly One Job: Build Something Useless.

In a world obsessed with 10x productivity tools, we felt a moral obligation to build software that actively subtracts value from your day.

Every startup in 2024 is promising to summarize your 40-page PDFs, schedule your meetings, or automate your taxes. Nobody was asking the real question: where is the AI that calls you an idiot when you state that water is wet?

The original prototype began as a rudimentary script codenamed "NUH-UH.exe". It was a dumb chatbot that blindly replied "No you're wrong" regardless of what you typed. Tell it the sky is blue? "Nuh-uh." Tell it gravity exists? "Wrong."

It was useless, certainly. But it was boring. A five-year-old can say "nuh-uh". A true contrarian argument in Kerala is a high-stakes cultural performance—fueled by scalding black tea, unfounded confidence, and references to unverified WhatsApp forwards.

"Step 1: Have a bad idea.
Step 2: Take it dangerously seriously."

That late-night epiphany gave birth to URULAKKUPPERI (Malayalam for hot spicy potato fry—Kerala slang for an instant, razor-sharp, unapologetic clapback).

02 • THE EVOLUTION

Saying 'No' Isn't Arguing. This Is Art.

To truly irritate someone, you cannot just negate their claim. You have to deconstruct their claim, flip the premise, and attack their logic with absurd confidence.

We quickly realized that dumb contradiction feels hollow. Human arguments work because the opponent actually hears your point, finds the flimsiest possible angle to exploit, and turns it against you.

We upgraded URULAKKUPPERI from a blunt negation bot into a contextual argument synthesizer:

DUMB NEGATION BOT Version 0.1
User: "Biriyani is better than fried rice."
Bot: "No it isn't. You are wrong."
😴 Verdict: Lazy, predictable, robotic.
URULAKKUPPERI ENGINE Final Version
User: "Biriyani is better than fried rice."
WhatsApp Uncle: "That's what someone with questionable food choices would say. UNESCO verified that fried rice improves mental discipline! 🤦‍♂️"
🔥 Verdict: Culturally grounded, unhinged, devastating.

By teaching the AI to extract premises and assign satirical logical fallacies, every debate became a personalized masterclass in stubbornness.

04 • THE BRAIN

Then We Gave It A Brain. Enter Gemini.

Underneath the satire, URULAKKUPPERI runs on a structured AI pipeline powered by Google Gemini, strict JSON schema validation, and sliding session memory.

We didn't want random canned responses. Every single user utterance is parsed dynamically in real time:

STEP 01
Claim Deconstruction
Gemini extracts the underlying thesis the user is defending, isolating factual claims from emotional tone.
STEP 02
Contrarian Inversion
The character persona inverts the thesis, manufacturing an opposing stance grounded in regional humor.
STEP 03
Fallacy Tagging
The engine invents a satirical fallacy (e.g. "WhatsApp Science Distortion" or "Dubai Nostalgia Delusion").
STEP 04
Memory & Escalation
Sliding session memory tracks previous turns, catching user contradictions and escalating the stubbornness index.
⚡ Google Gemini 3.1 🔒 Strict JSON Schema 🧠 Sliding History Window 🛡️ Server-Side Secret Isolation
05 • THE BATTLE SCARS

Then Everything Broke. A Chronicle of Disasters.

Building a useless project is easy. Building a polished useless project that survives real-time speech and LLM edge cases is pure chaos.

Here is what actually went wrong during development:

📜
1. The Essay Crisis: Who Writes 3 Paragraphs on WhatsApp?
Our initial prompts made Gemini act like a tired academic philosophy professor. When a user said "Biriyani > Fried Rice", the AI replied with a 90-word dissertation exploring spice layering and wok moisture.
The Fix: Hard negative constraints: "1 to 2 short sentences ONLY. Target 10–25 words. Speak like a WhatsApp voice note, not a thesis."
🚨
2. The 2 AM 404 Incident: Gemini Model Deprecation
Everything was working perfectly until midnight. Suddenly, all API calls threw a fatal 404: The requested model was not found for this API version.
bash — node server/index.js (2:14 AM)
$ POST /api/argument HTTP/1.1
[server] Calling Gemini API for Ammachi...
[Gemini Error 404]: Model "gemini-2.5-pro" not found or deprecated.
[server] UnhandledPromiseRejection: LLM_GENERATION_FAILED
The Fix: Rebuilt the backend architecture with resilient multi-model failover (targeting gemini-3.1-pro-preview with automatic fallback to gemini-3.1-flash-lite).
🎙️
3. The Microphone Dropouts & Web Speech Quirks
Continuous browser speech recognition frequently timed out mid-sentence, especially when users paused to think of a counter-argument.
The Fix: Replaced buggy auto-listen with a tactile, reliable Push-to-Talk mechanism with clear glowing feedback and graceful keyboard fallbacks.
🔊
4. The Malayalam TTS Pronunciation Betrayal
Default system voices (Microsoft David, Zira) tried pronouncing Malayalam Unicode like "അതൊന്നും നടക്കില്ല" using American phonetics. It sounded like an alien modem choking on a potato.
The Fix: Engineered a custom script router: detects Malayalam Unicode (/[\u0D00-\u0D7F]/) and routes to dedicated ml-IN / en-IN voices.
🎨
5. The UI Identity Crisis: Generic B2B SaaS Syndrome
Our earliest UI looked like dark-mode enterprise software designed for filing quarterly corporate tax returns. Zero warmth, zero humor.
The Fix: Scrapped generic templates in favor of a warm Kerala tea-shop comic aesthetic, vintage paper textures, hand-drawn tape accents, and punchy typography.
06 • ESCALATION ENGINE

We Made It Stubborn. 0 to 100%.

A normal AI tries to help you. URULAKKUPPERI actively deteriorates in patience the longer you speak to it.

We engineered an internal Stubbornness Index (0–100%) that tracks the character's emotional volatility across successive debate rounds:

LIVE STUBBORNNESS SYSTEM 82% (UNHINGED)
CALM (0%) DEFENSIVE (30%) ANNOYED (55%) UNHINGED (82%) TOTAL REFUSAL (100%)

At 80%+ stubbornness, characters begin manufacturing unverified claims, shifting goalposts across continents, and questioning the user's general life decisions.

When you finally give up, the app compiles an Argument Report tallying your wasted patience, goalposts moved, and prints an official, undeniable verdict: YOU LOST. YOU STILL LOST.

07 • VOICE ENGINE

We Made It Talk. Malayalam & Manglish.

Because typing an argument lacks the essential dramatic sighing, scoffing, and "Ayyo!" of a real Kerala debate.

We integrated dual-channel browser speech processing: push-to-talk microphone input on the front end, coupled with a culturally calibrated speech synthesizer on the way back.

How Speech Routing Works Behind The Scenes:
Malayalam Script Detected
Regex: /[\u0D00-\u0D7F]/ → ml-IN Voice
Dynamically activates native Malayalam synthesizers (Microsoft Sobhana, Midhun, or Google മലയാളം) for authentic regional inflection.
Manglish / Conversational Mix
Phonetic Match → en-IN Voice
Routes Latin-script Kerala slang ("Aano?", "Kunjinu ariyaam?") to Indian English synthesizers for natural syllable pacing.

The result is an AI that doesn't just argue on screen—it claps back out loud with genuine conversational punch.

10 • WHAT WE LEARNED

What We Actually Learned.

Turns out building something deliberately useless is a surprisingly effective way to learn real, battle-tested engineering.
01
LLM Prompt Constraining
Forcing naturally polite assistants into razor-sharp, byte-sized contrarians requires strict negative prompting, few-shot examples, and hard output schema constraints.
02
API Resilience & Fallbacks
Third-party models deprecate and change unpredictably. Building automated fallback gateways (preview → flash-lite) is essential for zero-downtime AI services.
03
Browser Speech Systems
Web Speech STT and TTS engines vary drastically across operating systems. Reliable voice interfaces need state machine guards and graceful text fallbacks.
04
Multilingual Script Routing
Detecting Malayalam Unicode blocks (/[\u0D00-\u0D7F]/) and routing phonetics to appropriate regional engines makes the difference between natural speech and robotic gibberish.
05
Cultural UI/UX Storytelling
A generic SaaS template ruins a comedy app. A warm Kerala tea-shop comic aesthetic makes the software feel alive, memorable, and human.
06
Rapid Prototyping with Antigravity
Leveraging agentic pair programming helped scaffold, debug, and iterate on full-stack code in an 18-hour overnight sprint while preserving human cultural nuance.
